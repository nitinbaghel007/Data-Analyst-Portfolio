# 🛍️ Retail Store Sales Intelligence & Customer Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes retail store sales data using SQL to generate business insights.  
The goal is to understand revenue trends, customer value, category performance, and discount impact.

---

## 📊 Dataset Description
The dataset contains:
- Customer ID
- Product Category
- Total Spent
- Discount Applied
- Year & Month
- Transaction Records

---

## 🛠️ SQL Analysis Performed

### 1️⃣ Total Records
Counts total transactions.

### 2️⃣ Total Revenue
Calculates overall revenue generated.

### 3️⃣ Monthly Revenue Trend
Analyzes revenue growth month-wise.

### 4️⃣ Running Revenue (Cumulative)
Tracks cumulative revenue over time.

### 5️⃣ Revenue by Category
Identifies top-performing categories.

### 6️⃣ Top 3 Revenue Contributing Categories
Finds categories contributing most to total revenue.

### 7️⃣ Customer Lifetime Value
Identifies top 5 high-value customers.

### 8️⃣ Customer Segmentation
Segments customers into Gold / Silver / Bronze based on spending.

### 9️⃣ Revenue by Discount Usage
Analyzes impact of discounts on revenue.

### 🔟 Yearly Revenue
Revenue performance year-wise.

### 1️⃣1️⃣ Average Order Value by Year
Calculates average spending per order.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- Identified high revenue categories.
- Recognized top customers contributing maximum revenue.
- Measured discount impact on total sales.
- Observed revenue growth trend over years.
